AI in Space Exploration Knowledge Hub

Educational & Technological Deep Dive

1. Autonomous Navigation (AutoNav)

Rovers on Mars like NASA's Perseverance use onboard AI stereo vision to continuously process 3D terrain maps while driving. It detects sharp boulders, deep sand traps, and cliffs, planning dynamic safe pathways without waiting for Earth operators.

2. AEGIS Target Selection

AEGIS (Autonomous Exploration for Gathering Increased Science) uses computer vision and machine learning on rover laser spectrometers to scan rocks, identify unique chemical signatures, and fire science lasers automatically.

3. Fault Detection & Diagnostics

Deep space probes use machine learning anomaly detection to monitor voltage spikes, wheel degradation, and thruster temp fluctuations. Self-healing software routes power away from faulty circuits before catastrophic failures occur.

4. Deep Space Neural Comms

AI neural image compression models filter out redundant background space noise and compress high-resolution scientific data up to 100x before transmission over long-distance Deep Space Network links.
